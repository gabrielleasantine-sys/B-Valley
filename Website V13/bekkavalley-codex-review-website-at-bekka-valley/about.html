The Story of Bekkã Valley

We, a nurse and an engineer, are food enthusiasts who love tweaking old world recipes handed down to us by our parents and grandparents. Infusing the recipes with secret ingredients to make them uniquely our own. Impressing many family and friends, who soon enough convinced us to start selling locally. As word about Bekka Valley grew and orders poured in, we looked at each other and said "We are going to need a bigger blender.!"

The rest is delicious history.

Thank you so much for enjoying our products and sharing them with your family and friends.

- Bekka Valley

Family Owned

Bekka Valley is a first generation, Lebanese-American woman owned company. We began with family-inspired Mediterranean recipes and a belief that everyday food should feel bright, healthy, and fun.

Our Process

We are proud to create the freshest hummus, garlic, pita chips and marinades made with real ingredients in our own facility.

Simple Ingredients

We prioritize chickpeas, tahini, citrus, herbs, and spices that deliver depth without overcomplication.

Organic

We do not use preservatives or genetically modified foods, just 6 real ingredients; chickpeas, tahini, lemon juice, salt, garlic and olive oil blend!

Shareable Flavor

Bekkā Valley products are made to bring people together — perfect for sharing around the table with friends and family. Whether it’s hummus, pita chips, or dressings, every bite is crafted to make gatherings more flavorful and memorable.
