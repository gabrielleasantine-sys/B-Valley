Pita Chips

Bekka Valley's Baked Sea Salt Pita Chips are the perfect compliment to our hummus and garlic dip. Each chip is hand cut and then hand tossed in our signature seasonings so that each chip is guaranteed to be packed with flavor. Then the pita chips are baked (never fried) to a golder brown color. Finally, as the pita chips are cooling, we finish them off with just the right amount of sea salt to create the perfect pita chip.
